Three reasons buyers call us first

Keep losing to cash offers?
A standard pre-approval doesn't tell a seller much. A fully underwritten loan does. It shows you can close on time, with no surprises, which is what sellers are really choosing between.
Waiting for rates to drop?
When rates fall, every buyer on the sidelines comes back at once and prices follow. A rate can be refinanced later. The price you pay for the house can't.
Tired of renting?
Most renters don't need 20% down. Many first-time buyer programs start at 3 to 3.5%, some veteran programs go to zero, and down payment assistance may be available. You may be closer than you think.
